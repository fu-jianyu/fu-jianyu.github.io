I presented **“Synergizing Mobility Data for Human Life Evolution in Real Spaces”** at IEEE BigData 2025 in Macau (8–11 December 2025). Our talk received the **Best Presentation Award** in the **4th Special Session on Dataspaces and Data Free Flow with Trust**.

The certificate recognizes Jianyu Fu, Zhihua Zhong, Xukang Wang, Jue Ma, and Yoshihide Sekimoto. The [Sekimoto Lab award announcement](https://sekilab.csis.u-tokyo.ac.jp/news/2025/6468) was posted on 17 December 2025.
