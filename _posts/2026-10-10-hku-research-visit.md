From **5 August to 10 October 2026**, I visited the **Department of Geography, The University of Hong Kong**, as a **visiting Ph.D. researcher**, hosted by **Prof. Bo Huang**.

During the visit, I conducted collaborative research on **urban mobility and human behavior**, focusing on spatial patterns of individual travel-time burdens in Greater Paris. The work combined a sanitized GNSS-based mobility dataset with urban spatial information to examine differences in observed travel experiences.

The related manuscript, _“When mobility continues at a cost: Individual travel-time burdens in Greater Paris,”_ is **under review**. I am grateful to Prof. Huang and the team for their support and discussions during the visit.
