Our paper **“Evaluating urban street views through collective emotional perception from multimodal social media cityscape posts: An R-ECoT-based interpretable framework”** was accepted by **Landscape and Urban Planning** on **23 October 2026**.

**JCR Q1 · Impact Factor 11.5**

Authors: **Jianyu Fu**, X. Wang, J. Ma, C. Zhao, Y. Ogawa, and Y. Sekimoto. I am the first and corresponding author.
