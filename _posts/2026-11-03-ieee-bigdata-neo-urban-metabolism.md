On **3 November 2026**, our paper **“Beyond Top-Down Urban Sensing: Cross-View Explanatory Asymmetry in Image-Estimated Streetscape Perception in Tokyo”** (SP06202) was **accepted for presentation** at the [Special Session on Neo Urban Metabolism](https://bigdataieee.org/BigData2026/calls/special-neo-urban-metabolism/) at **IEEE BigData 2026**. The China Computer Federation lists the main conference as [**CCF-C**](https://www.ccf.org.cn/Academic_Evaluation/Cross_Compre_Emerging/zgjsjxhtjgjxshy/cl/2019-05-14/665247.shtml).

The study asks whether a satellite view and a street-level view of the same Tokyo neighbourhood explain the same aspects of streetscape perception. We align the two perspectives on a 100-metre grid and compare how much each adds when the other is already available.
