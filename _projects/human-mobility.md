I study how urban form relates to human activity and accessibility using mobility data, geospatial analysis, and machine learning.

My first-authored paper, _“Linking Urban Morphology and Human Activity Patterns: An Empirical Study Based on Mobility Survey Data from the Paris Region,”_ was presented at **IEEE BigData 2025**. [Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/BigData66926.2025.11402166).

During my [2026 research visit to HKU]({% post_url 2026-10-10-hku-research-visit %}), I also investigated individual travel-time burdens in Greater Paris. The related manuscript is under review.
