Professional Summary

Ph.D. student in Socio-Cultural Environmental Studies at The University of Tokyo. My research focuses on urban perception, human mobility, urban accessibility, cityscape analytics, and AI-based urban modeling. I combine large-scale mobility data, geospatial analysis, computer vision, and multimodal artificial intelligence to support accessible, sustainable, and human-centered cities.
